## ✅ Booking Experience

Signed-in users can now plan, book, and track their fleet bookings from one place.

- **Plan a Trip:** Enter pickup, destination, dates, guest count, and budget.
- **Review Vehicle Options:** Get suggested fleet options based on the journey.
- **Book Securely:** Complete payment through Razorpay checkout.
- **Get Confirmation:** View a clean booking success page after payment.
- **Track Bookings:** Revisit past and active bookings from the booking history.
- **View Details:** Open any booking to see route, vehicle, payment status, and total amount.

---

## 🧭 How It Works

1. **Share the journey details** with pickup, drop, travel dates, and number of guests.
2. **Choose from suggested vehicles** matched to your route and requirement.
3. **Confirm the booking online** with a secure payment flow.
4. **Receive your booking summary** with vehicle, route, distance, amount, and status.
5. **Return anytime** to check your booking history and trip details.
